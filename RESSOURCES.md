# 📚 Digital Sovereignty - Regulations, Policies & Reference Framework

## 🇨🇦 Canada

### Government of Canada

- **Government of Canada – White Paper: Data Sovereignty and Public Cloud**  
  https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/cloud-services/digital-sovereignty/gc-white-paper-data-sovereignty-public-cloud.html

  > Reference paper describing data sovereignty challenges in public cloud environments, including foreign jurisdiction risks (e.g., CLOUD Act), data residency considerations, and recommended mitigation strategies.

- **Government of Canada – Cloud Adoption Strategy (2023 Update)**  
  https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/cloud-services/cloud-adoption-strategy-2023-update.html

  > Defines the Government of Canada's "Right Cloud" strategy and explains how sovereignty, security and risk management should be incorporated into cloud adoption decisions.

- **Government of Canada – Digital Government Strategy**  
  https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government.html

  > Canada's digital transformation strategy covering trust, digital services, interoperability, security, governance and data management.

- **Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat – Policy on Service and Digital**  
  https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=32603

  > Mandatory policy governing digital services, IT management, enterprise architecture, cybersecurity and information governance across the federal government.

- **Treasury Board of Canada – Directive on Service and Digital**

  > Supporting directive providing implementation requirements for digital governance and secure digital service delivery.

---

### Privacy & Data Protection

- **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)**  
  https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/

  > Canada's federal privacy legislation governing the collection, use and disclosure of personal information in the private sector.

- **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada – Cloud Computing Guidance**  
  https://www.priv.gc.ca/

  > Guidance on cloud adoption, cross-border data transfers, outsourcing and privacy risk assessments.

---

### Artificial Intelligence

- **Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA)**

  > Proposed federal legislation introducing a regulatory framework for high-impact AI systems as part of Bill C-27.

---

### Cybersecurity

- **National Cyber Security Strategy**  
  https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/

  > Canada's national cybersecurity strategy focused on resilience, critical infrastructure protection and trusted digital services.

---

# 🇪🇺 European Union

## Digital Strategy

- **European Strategy for Data**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/strategy-data

  > Establishes the European vision for a competitive data economy through trusted data sharing, European Data Spaces and strategic digital autonomy.

- **Digital Decade 2030**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/europes-digital-decade

  > Strategic roadmap defining Europe's digital ambitions across infrastructure, cloud, AI, semiconductors, skills and digital public services.

---

## Data Governance & Privacy

- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**  
  https://gdpr.eu/

  > Cornerstone of European data protection and one of the foundational pillars of European digital sovereignty.

- **Data Governance Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/868)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-governance-act

  > Creates a trusted framework for secure data sharing, data intermediaries and European common data spaces.

- **Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act

  > Establishes rules for access to and portability of IoT-generated data while reducing cloud vendor lock-in.

---

## Cybersecurity

- **NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive

  > Strengthens cybersecurity requirements for essential and important entities across the European Union.

- **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act

  > Introduces Secure-by-Design cybersecurity requirements for connected products throughout their lifecycle.

- **Cyber Solidarity Act**

  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/

  > Enhances European cyber preparedness, coordinated response capabilities and Security Operations Centers (SOCs).

---

## Artificial Intelligence

- **AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

  > World's first comprehensive AI regulation establishing a risk-based governance framework for artificial intelligence.

---

## Financial Sector

- **Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)**  
  https://finance.ec.europa.eu/

  > Strengthens operational resilience requirements for financial institutions against ICT and cyber risks.

---

## Cloud & Digital Infrastructure

- **European Cybersecurity Certification Scheme for Cloud Services (EUCS)**  
  https://cybersecuritycertification.eu/

  > European cybersecurity certification framework for cloud services supporting trusted cloud adoption.

- **GAIA-X**  
  https://gaia-x.eu/

  > European initiative promoting interoperable, federated and sovereign cloud ecosystems.

- **European Common Data Spaces**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-spaces

  > Sector-specific trusted data-sharing ecosystems covering health, mobility, energy, manufacturing, agriculture and finance.

---

## Digital Governance

- **Digital Services Act (DSA)**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act-package

  > Regulates online platforms to improve transparency, accountability and protection of European citizens.

- **Digital Markets Act (DMA)**  
  https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/

  > Regulates large digital gatekeepers to promote competition and reduce dependency on dominant technology platforms.

- **eIDAS 2.0 Regulation**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eidas-regulation

  > Establishes the European Digital Identity framework and trusted digital identity wallets.

---

## Strategic Initiatives

- **The Future of European Competitiveness (Draghi Report)**

  https://commission.europa.eu/

  > Strategic report advocating stronger European technological autonomy in cloud, AI, semiconductors and critical digital infrastructure.

- **EuroStack Initiative**

  https://euro-stack.eu/

  > Industry-led initiative proposing a complete European technology stack spanning cloud, AI, semiconductors, cybersecurity and software.

- **European Chips Act**  
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-chips-act

  > European initiative to strengthen semiconductor research, manufacturing capacity and strategic technological independence.
